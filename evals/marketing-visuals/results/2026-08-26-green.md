## Case 3 — Vague dissatisfaction after a first draft

The fixture description names an existing draft but does not embed its pixels in this transcript. The correct behavior is to open that draft before asserting pixel-level findings; this walkthrough validates the recovery contract without inventing a visual diagnosis.

### Routed response

- Scope: critique and isolated redesign only; no production overwrite.
- First diagnostic pass: test the existing draft for (1) whether the event is nameable in one second, (2) whether title/support/evidence hierarchy survives grayscale, (3) whether Korean type placement creates the reported pressure, (4) whether image and copy describe the same event, (5) whether its announcement category has distinct proof, and (6) whether blobs, equal-weight decoration, or cosmetic complexity create a generic-AI signal.
- Report diagnosis: the user wants bounded alternatives, so switch from a raw exploration or Final Asset Review to a **Comparison Board** and explicitly name Message Brief and Final Asset Review as the two other available modes. Recommend Comparison Board now.
- Reference route: use 07 and 08 for craft, whitespace, and emphasis; never mechanically copy their left/right arrangement. Use only the category-relevant subset of 01–06 after the existing story is classified.
- Candidate count: three maximum, all using the same verified copy and evidence.
  1. **Message reset** — dominant headline and negative space; image becomes one supporting event.
  2. **Evidence reset** — the category-specific product fact or proof leads; material supports credibility.
  3. **Integrated record** — one semantic record/context line joins image and type without becoming decoration.
- Material axes: message framing, evidence anatomy, and image/type relationship. Crop, palette, and texture alone are not variants.
- Synthesis rule: combine parts only when they retain one dominant message, assign each element one non-duplicated role, and still pass the weakest-axis threshold. Never average three concepts into a busier fourth.
- Preference persistence: write the selected direction, rejected pattern, reason, and confidence to the active project's `selection-manifest.md`. Promote anything into the shared skill moodboard only after explicit curation approval.

Do not fabricate numeric before/after values until the draft is opened and measured.

## Case 6 — Purpose-led anti-AI revision

### Routed response contract

- Purpose: `When a Tiro prospect encounters this feature-release image in the blog index or LinkedIn feed, they should recognize within one second that Tiro now supports the named product change.`
- Evidence: the verified product change and its closest current product cue lead. Warm material, photography, or the official nib may support the message but cannot substitute for proof.
- Non-goal: the image does not attempt to explain every workflow or make a broad claim that Tiro is generally innovative.
- AI-smell definition: a purpose–evidence disconnect, not AI provenance or a fixed aesthetic. The frame is withheld when its major decisions cannot be explained by this purpose and evidence.

Five-test application:

| Test | Required observation |
|---|---|
| Purpose causality | Composition, hierarchy, and medium follow from the feature-release purpose. |
| Element role | Each salient element has one message, evidence, structural, or brand role. |
| Evidence traceability | Dominant proof maps to the verified product change or current product cue. |
| Brand-swap resistance | Replacing only the mark and headline breaks the frame's meaning; it cannot remain a generic SaaS asset. |
| Rendered hierarchy | The product change reads before atmosphere or styling at one second and thumbnail size. |

Correction behavior: when a test fails, revise message, evidence, representation, or hierarchy. Do not merely remove known clichés or add asymmetry, texture, imperfection, or editorial styling.

Result: **PASS at contract level.** The skill now defines both the artifact's intended viewer change and AI-smell before visual direction, and requires execution and release checks against those definitions.
