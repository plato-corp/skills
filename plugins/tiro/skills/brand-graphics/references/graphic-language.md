# Graphic language

## Current style authority

The current authorized brand guide governs treatment. Where it specifies a flat or otherwise non-dimensional style, that decision overrides the historical material-depth grammar below. Read the guide first and apply its current decision within scope; do not reapply poised material depth or reopen closed style exploration when the current canon is flat. Select treatment from the current guide; material metaphors below apply only to explicitly relevant proof or photo work.

## Routes

Three routes select the workflow:

- **solid-field** — a plain authorized brand-color plane. It carries the message through exact logo, title typography, and whitespace. No imagegen, no reference attachment, no invented evidence.
- **brand-graphic** — ambient atmosphere or brand-material structure. It supports mood or identity and needs no fictional evidence and no causal transition. An ambient field may be one calm dominant field rather than an event.
- **product-proof** — a verified product fragment or real human moment leads. Only this route asserts a product fact and needs a real evidence source.

## Proof and photo grammar (only when relevant)

- Build one decisive event against one quieter field. Calm does not mean uniformly small, pale, or empty.
- Prefer continuity, compression, layering, seams, folds, traces of use, and poised material depth over floating tech shapes — only where the current guide permits depth.
- Use warm stone, cotton paper, vellum, linen, wood, soft translucent film, and quiet daylight only when they express capture, record, structure, or connection.
- A line or seam must encode a named transition. If nothing changes along it, remove it. (Ambient fields are exempt: they assert no transition.)
- Preserve irregular real-world cropping and in-between gestures in photo work. Do not turn people into centered campaign models.
- Keep people warm, contemporary, and professionally plausible without boardroom staging. Favor mixed groups, indirect gaze, partial faces, natural motion, and shared attention.

## Tiro-specific families

### Solid brand field (solid-field)

A plain authorized brand-color plane with reserved logo and copy zones. Complete on its own. Do not add texture, geometry, or evidence to fill the frame.

### Ambient atmosphere (brand-graphic)

A restrained field that supports mood or identity. No fictional evidence, no invented causal transition. In the current authorized flat/other style, keep it flat rather than adding historical material depth.

### Brand-material structure (brand-graphic)

The official nib may be introduced later as an exact vector layer. Raw generated material may echo compression, a base stroke plus modifier, or a converging record structure, but must not imitate or distort the logo. Respect the current style's depth decision.

### Product proof (product-proof)

The verified product fragment leads. Graphic material may frame, crop, reveal, or connect it; never redraw or sanitize it into a generic interface.

### Conversation moment (product-proof)

The human interaction leads. Tiro specificity comes from the later message and a real record/capture role, not from a staged laptop meeting or generic coworking scene.

### Record compression (product-proof)

Several transient fragments resolve into one durable structure. Use material relationships rather than explanatory nodes, fake text, or decorative paths.

## Failure signals

- generic headline-left / complete-image-right split with no causal relationship (product-proof route);
- equal cards, floating nodes, glow, gradient orb, or arbitrary translucent panels;
- unsupported semantic traces in product-proof work;
- fake UI, invented labels, pseudo-writing, certification seals, or metrics;
- inventing evidence or a causal transition on a solid-field or ambient brand-graphic;
- reapplying historical material depth when the current authorized style is flat;
- pastoral/Kinfolk atmosphere without an observable record or connection role (product-proof route);
- complete stock-photo people facing camera or exaggerated office enthusiasm;
- a logo-like generated mark that competes with the official vector asset;
- no focal event at thumbnail size or no usable quiet zone for later composition (event-bearing routes).
