# Reports and quality

Use for material alternatives, dissatisfaction recovery, candidate release, and final handoff.

## Candidate count

Produce one polished direction when message, category, and evidence anatomy are clear.

Produce 2–3 only when at least one material axis differs:

- one-second message framing;
- evidence anatomy;
- image/type relationship;
- degree of product literalness versus brand metaphor;
- information density or channel strategy.

Crop, color, texture, or decoration alone do not justify a new candidate. Raw vocabulary boards may contain more exploratory images only when the user explicitly asks for breadth; label them as exploration, not final candidates.

## Complete composition contract

Every candidate contains:

- the real or clearly labeled sample headline;
- editable category, support, and evidence copy;
- the visual asset in its intended crop;
- the actual type hierarchy and line breaks;
- a restrained Tiro signature;
- requested channel or aspect-ratio behavior.

A generated background without type is an ingredient, not a candidate.

## Three report modes

### Message Brief

Use when the message, claim, source, or category is unclear.

Include: source receipt, one-second message, facts/assumptions, category route, evidence anatomy, reference route, recommended intervention, and claims to verify. Do not create cosmetic options before the brief resolves the decision.

### Comparison Board

Use for 2–3 material alternatives. All options use the same verified copy, evidence, fixture, channel, and edge cases. Name each decision axis and explain what each option optimizes. Keep the report isolated from production.

### Final Asset Review

Use after direction confirmation or an explicit final request. Include the complete composition, optical change log, copy/evidence source mapping, crop behavior, scorecard, export checklist, and remaining verification.

The provided `assets/report-shell.html` is a starting scaffold. Replace its fallback variables with verified current tokens when available. Keep report chrome quiet: one reading axis, one compact header, actions grouped once, navigation only for long reports, no nested card stacks, repeated button rows, decorative filters, or competing sticky regions.

## Dissatisfaction recovery

When the user says “이게 아닌데,” seems stuck, or cannot name the problem:

1. diagnose message clarity, hierarchy, type placement, image-to-copy relationship, evidence fit, category distinction, and generic-AI patterns;
2. separate rejection of the report shape from rejection of the visual direction;
3. name the two alternative report modes and recommend one switch;
4. use references 07 and 08 as the craft bar without copying their layout;
5. return 2–3 corrected directions only when the diagnosis identifies material axes;
6. state the synthesis rule when combining preferred parts;
7. persist newly confirmed taste signals in the project's selection manifest.

## Quality gate

Choose 4–6 criteria specific to the artifact. Common candidates:

- purpose legibility: the intended viewer change is observable in the named channel and time window;
- one-second message clarity;
- category and evidence distinction;
- image-to-type relationship;
- Tiro specificity;
- typography and Korean line breaks;
- claim integrity;
- operational reuse across requested channels.

For every selected criterion, record:

```text
Criterion: <name>
10/10 means: <observable target for this asset>
Score: <0–10 with evidence>
Correction to reach 10: <concrete change>
```

Iterate until every critical score is at least 9.0. An average cannot hide a weak criterion. Self-score is a release gate, not proof of audience success; real channel performance and user validation remain separate.

### Purpose-led AI-smell gate

Do not score “not AI-like” as taste or surface polish. Record evidence for:

- **Purpose causality:** major choices follow from the purpose rather than a style menu.
- **Element role:** every salient element has one message, evidence, structural, or brand role.
- **Evidence traceability:** dominant proof maps to a verified source.
- **Brand-swap resistance:** replacing only the logo and headline would break the composition's meaning.
- **Rendered hierarchy:** the intended viewer change survives one-second and thumbnail inspection.

Fail and revise when any dominant element cannot justify its existence, or when the composition remains plausible for another SaaS or lifestyle brand after a logo/headline swap. Removing blobs, cards, glows, or random labels is not sufficient. Adding asymmetry, texture, imperfection, or editorial styling is not sufficient either.

Diagram checks when applicable:

- each frame has a declared role and one relationship it proves;
- the chosen grammar matches the relationship: tree, branch, boundary, transition, process, comparison, or verified product fragment;
- every connector, group, and boundary has a named semantic role;
- equal cards or repeated nodes appear only for genuinely equal entities;
- mixed-media series are unified by typography, spacing, palette, trace logic, and signature rather than medium repetition;
- atmospheric photography never substitutes for hierarchy, transition, permission, containment, or visibility.
- no text overlaps, clips, or escapes its frame at the actual export size;
- connector endpoints stop cleanly at their intended node or boundary and never protrude through text;
- type size, type weight, stroke weight, and spacing form one hierarchy rather than competing for emphasis;
- visual inspection passes at both thumbnail and actual export size; numeric structure checks cannot approve a diagram by themselves.

Universal checks:

- the purpose statement names one audience, channel, intended change, and time window rather than a style or deliverable;
- hierarchy works through type, proportion, and space before borders or color;
- every element has a message, evidence, structural, or brand role;
- every dominant visual decision can be traced to the purpose or verified evidence, and the frame fails if it survives a Tiro-to-generic brand swap unchanged;
- no unsupported claim, metric, badge, logo, date, or partner appears;
- image and type remain editable and traceable to sources;
- Korean tracking is zero or negative, wrapping is word-based, and optical changes are logged;
- channel adaptations preserve message priority;
- the official Tiro nib is not rejected as a literal-origin prop, while generic quills, Roman props, and writing nostalgia remain excluded;
- any nib-originating trace maps to an actual movement among speech, capture, record, structure, and sharing rather than ornamental motion;
- a photo-led candidate shows an observable Tiro role beyond pastoral atmosphere; warm interiors alone are Kinfolk drift, not brand specificity;
- no gradients, cold-tech clichés, generic literal-origin props, or generic AI decoration;
- the declared stage is finished rather than handed off at 90%.
