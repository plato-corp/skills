# Reference premise

Read this module for `study` or an unresolved visual premise. A supplied approved source fixes the premise through `fidelity-port` and bypasses this module.

Current Tiro family evidence may satisfy the reference gate when verified sibling pages and the active design system already establish the needed craft ceiling, category behavior, and technique. Record each internal reference's job and visible translation. Perform external search or request attachments only when those current sources cannot resolve a material direction decision; lack of external inspiration alone is not a pause condition in Autonomous mode.

Ask for 1–5 liked screenshots when the user has them. Otherwise perform one focused search through the best available real-product source: `lazyweb-search-screens`, [Refero Styles](https://styles.refero.design/), or [Oh My Design Builder](https://oh-my-design.kr/builder). Use the source that can expose the relevant page family, complete flow, or technique; do not search all catalogs by default. Shortlist 2–4 references with distinct roles:

- **Canon:** independently recognized or repeatedly respected work that sets the craft ceiling.
- **Category:** a page solving a similar visitor and product problem.
- **Technique:** one strong motion, product scene, typographic, photographic, or structural mechanism.

Industry reputation is a discovery prior and quality ceiling, not proof that a layout fits Tiro. User-nominated work is preference evidence even when external acclaim is unverified. A prestigious but weakly relevant source may calibrate restraint or finish; a less famous but highly relevant source may supply one tactical mechanism.

Give every selected reference one job:

```text
Reference: what was actually inspected
Why it is useful: reputation, category fit, or technique
Extraction: one strength to retain
Tiro translation: how the strength changes for this page
Do not copy: surface or behavior to reject
Result: where the translation will be visible
```

Do not remove a strong reference merely because an earlier translation failed. Correct the extraction and make the lineage visible. If neither supplied references nor useful research is available, stop at the brief and invite attachments.

An implemented section, polished card, or inherited site surface does not satisfy the reference gate and is not evidence that its carrier or color climate was approved. Reuse those decisions only when the current approval record names the reference extraction, primary carrier, and dominant color distribution. Otherwise re-open the smallest decisive scene before polishing the existing implementation.

## 4. Choose the carrier and component language

Choose exactly one primary message carrier:

- **Photo:** a credible person, moment, or environment is the evidence.
- **Product state:** a real before/after state or retained artifact proves the claim.
- **Graphic:** a relationship, boundary, transformation, or system must be explained.
